# 2026-09-08 — the outreach pipeline rebuilt

## What changed

Six commits, `e73722b` to `c817c6b`, plus `1a69e0a` for the tests.

- **All generated copy is gone.** Thirty cold bodies and forty referral bodies are replaced by
  ten fixed templates each, generated from ColdEmail's `henry_cold_email_templates_10.md` and
  `henry_referral.md` by `scripts/build_templates.py`. `outreach_templates.gs` is generated —
  do not hand-edit it. The Company tab's own three message columns went with them.
- **Caps:** cold 20 a weekday (weekends still zero), referral 10, follow-up **0 and switched
  off**, mailbox budget 30.
- **A `Sent` column** on both contact tabs, written by the senders after Gmail accepts. It is
  the durable half of "nobody hears from us twice"; the queue fingerprint is the other half.
- **A `From` column**: 1 the original pipeline, 2 the first treg run, 3 the second.
- **Target companies are refused by the cold path**, as they already were by referral.

## What was done to the live sheets

- `Contacts (seed)` replaced: 3,021 rows, 756 companies, 284 ticked Sent.
- `Employees (seed)` replaced: 2,144 rows, 231 companies, 30 ticked Sent.
- `Email` (317 rows) and `Referral` (255 rows) cleared, after `markAlreadySentContacts` moved
  what they knew onto the contact tabs. Copies kept as hidden `Email archive 2026-09-08` and
  `Referral archive 2026-09-08`.

**Order matters if this is ever repeated:** tick first, clear second. Those two tabs were the
only record of who had been written to.

## Still open

- **Nothing is sending.** All three modes are `Pause` and no trigger is installed. Turning it on
  is a deliberate act somebody still has to take.
- **The harness is red in the working tree and green at `59ebe88`.** This is the fact most
  worth carrying forward, and it took until the end of the day to establish. Running the tests
  in the working tree gives 564 ok / 31 failed; checking out `59ebe88` into a clean worktree
  gives **633 ok, 0 failures**. The 31 are therefore caused by the uncommitted changes described
  below, not by anything in the repository's history — and the "no new failures" bar used all
  day was measured against an already-broken tree.

  Whoever picks that work up should know their tree is red, and that it was green when they
  started.

- **`harness.js` is the one file where the split failed.** The six source commits were separated
  content-by-content so that none carries somebody else's work. `harness.js` could not be:
  both parties rewrote large parts of it, and `1a69e0a` therefore carries their new tests as
  well as mine. The visible consequence is that a clean checkout of HEAD runs 571 ok / 24
  failed, and all 24 are their tests for features whose source is still uncommitted — the
  removed weekly company cap, the twenty-minute cold draft fallback, the Target-and-recency
  referral gate, the broadened Verify policy.

  So HEAD is red for a reason that resolves itself the moment that work is committed, and the
  right fix is to commit it rather than to touch those tests.
- **A large working tree of somebody else's unfinished changes** sits alongside this: roughly
  thirty files under `job_sync/`, `tests/`, `docs/`, plus edits inside `outreach_config.gs`,
  `outreach_send.gs`, `outreach_referral_send.gs`, `outreach_referral_queue.gs` and
  `target_tab.gs`. Today's commits were staged content-by-content to keep those out.

  One of them bit: `outreach_queue.gs` first called `outreachReferralTargetCompanies_` and
  `outreachReferralCompanyIsTarget_` to decide Target membership, and both exist only in that
  uncommitted work — so the commit built in the working tree and crashed on checkout. The cold
  path now reads the tab itself. **That duplication is temporary**: when the referral work
  lands, the two readers must be collapsed into one.
- **Subject lines run long.** Seven of ten cold and nine of ten referral subjects pass the sixty
  characters a phone shows. That is the copy's business, and the counts are asserted so the
  number cannot drift unnoticed.
- The referral message no longer prints a posting link, because the templates say the job ID
  will be sent afterwards. The tab still refuses to send a row without one.
