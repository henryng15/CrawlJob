# 2026-09-17 — Referral-database coverage audit

## Four-week gap audit

- The owner asked which employers applied to in the last four weeks have people in the *referral*
  database (`Employees (seed)`) but have had neither a cold email nor a referral ask sent, and
  nothing queued. The existing coverage audits could not answer it: they read `Contacts (seed)`,
  and recruiter coverage says nothing about whether there is somebody to ask for a referral.
- Added three read-only probes to `outreach_diag.gs` — `auditRecentReferralCandidatesWithoutOutreach`,
  its flat `report…` wrapper, and `reportOutboundToCompanyContacts`. Documented in
  [`docs/components/outreach-diagnostics.md`](../docs/components/outreach-diagnostics.md).
  Nothing was written to any sheet, queue, campaign property, trigger or mailbox.
- Live result for 2026-08-21 through 2026-09-17: 766 applications across 491 employers, 50 of which
  have referral contacts on file. Eight looked untouched on the sheets; Gmail cleared two of them.
- **Real gaps (4):** Rivian (12 referral contacts, 18 recruiters, two active applications Sep 7–8),
  Toast (2 / 8, applied Sep 1), Activision (2 / 0, applied Sep 11) and Northrop Grumman (10 / 0,
  one active Linthicum application Sep 3 among four rejections).
- **Reported but not gaps:** Wells Fargo is untouched only because its ten pending entries were
  cancelled on Sep 16 at the owner's request while both applications are in process. S&P Global's
  only application in the window is `Rejected`.

## Seed Sent ticks missing for two hand-run batches

- Adobe and Epic Games read as untouched on every sheet and property, and Gmail then showed nine
  referral asks to Adobe on Sep 3 (`sendRemainingAdobeReferralBatch`) and one to Epic Games on
  Sep 1. Those one-off senders write no queue row and tick no `Employees (seed)` `Sent` cell, so
  all ten recipients are still counted as available and the referral planner would ask them again.
- Left uncorrected and reported to the owner rather than fixed here: ticking `Sent` spends a
  contact permanently, which is not a change to make inside a read-only audit.
