# Referral Email Templates - Summer 2027 SWE Outreach

These 10 templates use the same structure:
- Humble opening that acknowledges the unexpected outreach
- **A bit about me** with four skimmable bullets
- **Why I'm reaching out** with the role fit
- **A quick question** asking about a referral without pressure
- Polite resume and closing lines

The subjects are deliberately split between `Referral request` and `Referral ask`
so sequential templates do not repeat the same opening phrase.

## Template 1

**Subject:** Referral request - Student SWE with backend reliability experience

Hi [Name],

I’m sorry for contacting you out of the blue, especially since we haven’t met before, but I recently came across [Company]’s opening for **[Role Name]**. After reading the job description, I felt the role could be a strong match for my background.

**A bit about me**

> - I’m a **Math & CS student at Kenyon College (4.0 GPA)** looking for a Summer 2027 software engineering internship.
> - At **Mined XAI**, I built a crash-risk search assistant using a **Python MCP server and DuckDB**, cutting analyst research from **30 minutes to 1 minute**.
> - I built an **AWS Fargate/S3** data-quality pipeline that validated **3.2M records** and blocked **340K malformed rows**.
> - At **Vietcombank**, I deployed a **Kubernetes/PostgreSQL** registration service that enforced seat limits across **500 concurrent requests with no overbookings**.

**Why I’m reaching out**

> The role’s focus on backend reliability and infrastructure felt closely aligned with the work I’ve been doing.

**A quick question**

> If my background seems relevant, would you feel comfortable referring me for the role? If so, I’d be happy to send over the job ID afterward. If not, I completely understand.

I’ve attached my resume for your reference.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I hope the rest of your day goes well.

Best,  
Henry Nguyen
